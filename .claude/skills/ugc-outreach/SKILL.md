# UGC Outreach — getting paid per video

| Path | Pays | Depends on views? |
|---|---|---|
| **UGC for brands** | **$150–400/video** | **No** |
| TikTok Shop affiliate | 13% avg | Yes |
| Amazon Associates | 1–3%, cut 50% in 2026 | Yes |

Jon's last month of Amazon affiliate income: **$6.46** on 8 orders.
Four UGC videos at $195: **$780**. That is ~120x, and it does not require a
single view.

He already makes product videos daily. He is giving that skill away for
**$0.81 per sale.**

## What Jon is selling

Not his audience. **The videos.** Brands run them as their own paid ads.

His actual, provable assets:
- 4.6K Instagram following (credibility, not the product)
- ~12,000 YouTube views in 23 days on a 14-subscriber channel
- Retention watch ratio above 1.0 across the full curve — viewers re-watch
- A proven home/kitchen/organization niche with an 11.76% click-to-order rate

That conversion number is the strongest thing in the pitch. Lead with it.

## Finding brands

Target sellers in Jon's proven categories: home, kitchen, organization,
cleaning, pets. Best fits are brands that:
- Sell on Amazon and/or TikTok Shop
- Have **weak or no video** on their listings
- Are small-to-mid sized (a household name will not answer a cold email)
- Make something with a **visible mechanism** — it collapses, dispenses,
  transforms. That is what Jon's top-clicked products all have in common.

Use the Apollo connector to find marketing and ecommerce contacts at those
companies. Search titles like "ecommerce manager", "brand manager", "marketing
manager", "founder" at consumer-products companies in home and kitchen.

Use `apollo-sequences` to run the campaign once a list exists.

## The pitch

Short. Specific. Proof up front. No flattery.

> Subject: video for {{product}}
>
> Hi {{first_name}} — I make product videos for home and kitchen brands.
>
> I ran {{product_category}} content to an 11.76% click-to-order rate last
> month, and my last 20 videos averaged over 100% view-through, meaning people
> re-watch them.
>
> I noticed {{product}} doesn't have video on the listing. I'd make you one —
> product-only, no talking head, 8–15 seconds, shot for Reels/TikTok/Shorts.
>
> $200 for the first one so you can see if it performs. If it does, 5 for $800.
>
> Want me to send two examples in your category?
>
> — Jon, Prime Pick Daily

Rules:
- Name the actual product
- One real number, not three
- Ask a small question, not for a call
- Never claim followers he does not have
- Never promise performance

## Weekly cadence

Five pitches per Sunday. At a typical 5–10% cold reply rate that is roughly one
conversation a fortnight, compounding as the portfolio grows.

Track in `primepick/ugc/pipeline.md`: brand, product, contact, date sent,
reply, outcome.
