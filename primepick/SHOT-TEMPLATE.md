# The 4.2M Template

Reverse-engineered from @bymichellelei's Amazon kitchen reel
(`instagram.com/reel/DbbB19MpUky/`) — **4.2M views, 79x her own median**,
402K followers. 12.8 seconds, 7 shots, 6 cuts.

---

## Two corrections to earlier advice

**1. The comment gate is CAPTION-ONLY.** There is no on-screen CTA anywhere
in this video. Not one frame. The video stays a pure product demo start to
finish; "write TRAY & I'll share the 🔗" lives only in the caption.

Earlier I said to put the gate both on screen and in the caption. The
4.2M example says otherwise — **do not put the CTA in the video.** Asking
on screen costs you a beat of demo time and breaks the spell.

**2. It does not loop.** Shot 7 ends on a close-up of the handle; shot 1
opens wide on the counter. No loop-back. Don't force one.

## The rules this reveals

### Lead with the most dramatic function, not the main one
This is a **cutting board.** It opens with **ice melting.** They led with the
most visually impossible-looking function, not the primary use case.

Ask of every product: *what does it do that looks like a magic trick?*
That is shot 1, regardless of what the product is "for."

### ~2 seconds per shot, cuts on the beat
Six of seven shots run almost exactly 2s. Shot 6 is 1s — the fastest cut
lands on the tightest close-up. Every cut falls on a musical beat.

### Text style
- **Lowercase serif** for feature lines — not bold sans, not all-caps
- Only the hook uses caps (`KITCHEN FIND`) plus a script accent (`Amazon`)
- Position **rotates**: lower-center → upper-center → lower-center → center-left → upper-center
- One idea per card, phrased as a plain feature, no hype

### Audio
Chill mid-tempo instrumental with vocal chops. Starts on the opening frame.
Beat drop at 0:02 carries the first cut. **No voiceover at all.** Music
cuts out at the end.

### Framing ladder
Wide → medium close → wide → medium close → high-angle close → extreme
close → detail. It **tightens** as it goes. The last three shots are the
tightest, riding the fastest cuts.

## Your 10-second adaptation

| Shot | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:02 | **The magic trick.** Most dramatic function, mid-action on frame one. Hook text. |
| 2 | 0:02–0:04 | Reveal what it is. Product name text. |
| 3 | 0:04–0:06 | Feature one, in use. |
| 4 | 0:06–0:08 | Feature two, tighter framing. |
| 5 | 0:08–0:10 | Detail shot. Final feature. |

Five shots, 2s each, cuts on the beat, framing tightens throughout,
lowercase serif text rotating position, music only, **no CTA on screen.**

The gate word goes in the caption and nowhere else.

## Caption template

```
[First-person hook line, same voice as your titles]

[One sentence on what it actually does]

Write "[WORD]" and I'll send you the link 🔗

#amazonfinds #[category] #[niche] #[broad]
```

Real example from the 4.2M reel:
> "we love a multi use tool in the kitchen 🫶🏼 write "TRAY" & I'll share the 🔗!"

Short. Casual. The gate is the last thing before hashtags.
