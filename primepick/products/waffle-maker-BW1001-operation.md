## Sequence
1. **Preheat.** Turn dial: OFF / SPECIALTY / 1-5. Right pill light glows **red** while
   heating. Ready = **three beeps** + left pill light turns **green**.
2. **Fill.** Machine stays CLOSED and UPRIGHT. Batter poured into a **permanently open
   rectangular funnel** on top (a small fill-line marking on its front lip). No lid, no
   flap, nothing opened or closed. Cup filled to the line (~1 cup), poured in one
   continuous pour.
3. **Cook.** Red light on. **Faint steam rises from the top funnel.** Nothing moves.
4. **Done.** Three beeps, green light. **Does NOT open by itself.**
5. **Open.** Thumb **presses a release button on the left-side handle**, hand **pulls the
   front plate forward and down**. Bottom hinge, swings ~90°, ends **lying flat
   horizontal** toward the user. Rear plate stays vertical.
6. **Waffle position.** Resting horizontally on the opened front plate (YouTube review and
   listing photo). One TikTok shows it leaning on the back plate — minority; use front.
7. **Remove.** Tongs (TikTok, listing photo) or a flat spatula (YouTube). Releases cleanly.
8. **Close.** Handle pushed back up, **latches with a click**.

## The finished waffle
Thick rectangle, ~1 inch, four equal quadrants, deep square pockets, golden. **A small
tab of extra cooked batter on one edge** where batter filled the funnel neck.

## Overfill (verified, usable as a future hook)
Excess batter rises into the top funnel like a tray and cooks there, attached to the
waffle. Does not spill onto the counter.

## What the first render got wrong
- Front plate opened on its own — WRONG, a hand must release and pull it down
- No release-button press, no 90° drop to flat
- No steam from the funnel, no batter tab

## Never render
- The machine opening by itself
- A top lid lifting up, or a clamshell flip
- Readable text on the funnel marking (AI garbles it) — keep it subtle and illegible
