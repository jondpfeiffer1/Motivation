## 1. The comment gate — fix your #1 problem with one line

Your biggest weakness is **zero comments**. 2,789 views → 23 likes → 0 comments.
Comments are the strongest ranking signal on Shorts and Reels.

**Four of the six top Instagram outliers use the same mechanic:**

| Creator | Views | vs. their median | The line |
|---|---|---|---|
| @bymichellelei | **4.2M** | 79x | "write **TRAY** & I'll share the 🔗!" |
| @sam.shan.shops | **72M** | 18.9x | "**WRITE: KITCHEN** for top 5 no regret finds" |
| @tiffany_williamson | **1.4M** | 21.1x | "**Comment 'link'** and I'll DM you" |
| @dr_inlove_ | **384K** | 80.3x | "Follow & **comment down for the link**" |

Instead of "link in bio," you ask for a **specific word** in the comments.

This fixes two problems at once:
1. Every person who wants the product now leaves a comment → ranking signal
2. A DM is far higher intent than hoping they find a link in your bio

**Your current CTA — "Link in bio!" — asks for zero engagement.** That single
phrase is costing you both the comments and the clicks.

### Pick a word tied to the product
`TRAY` · `SLUSH` · `LITTER` · `MOP` · `PUMP` — short, all caps, one word,
easy to type. Ask for it in the caption AND as an on-screen text line.

### ⚠️ Compliance guardrail
Amazon's Operating Agreement **prohibits affiliate links in email**, and DMs
are an unsettled area. Do NOT paste a raw tagged link into a DM.
**Send them to your bio.site page or Amazon Storefront instead.** You keep
the whole engagement mechanic and stay clean.

## 3. The first 3 seconds — copy this structure

Every outlier opens the same way: **the product already mid-action**, with a
text hook on screen. No intro, no face, no "hey guys."

| Creator | Opening visual | Text on screen |
|---|---|---|
| @bymichellelei | Ice cubes melting instantly on a tray | "Amazon KITCHEN FIND" |
| @findswithmia | Vacuum inhaling confetti off a rug | "POV: The first time you deep clean your carpet 🥺" |
| @tomahawkbbq | Seasoning dumped on raw steak | "This will save your marriage" |
| @shop.with.karinn | Rice pouring into a dispenser | "He didn't believe her" |

### The four hook types that work
1. **Instant proof** — the mechanism working in frame one (ice melting, confetti vanishing)
2. **Stakes** — "This will save your marriage"
3. **Doubt / conflict** — "He didn't believe her"
4. **POV** — "POV: you stop mopping forever"

You already write #4 well. Add the other three.

**Rule: the product must be visibly doing its thing before the first second
is over.** No slow reveal, no context-setting.
