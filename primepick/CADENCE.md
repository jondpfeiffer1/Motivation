## How these videos make money

A video with no path to a tagged link earns nothing. Every run ships all four:

1. **Gate word** in the caption only — converts "link in bio" (zero engagement)
   into a comment. Comments are the top ranking signal on Shorts and Reels, and
   the account is currently at **zero**.
2. **`/<slug>` landing page** on primepickdailystore.lovable.app — one product,
   one button, above the fold.
3. **Click tracking** via sendBeacon — the funnel is finally measurable.
   ~12,000 views produced 68 Amazon clicks (~0.5%); we now find out why.
4. **Tagged link** carrying `pfeiffer2-20`.
