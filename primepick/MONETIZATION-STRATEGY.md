# Monetization Strategy — where the money actually is

Researched 14 Sep 2026.

---

## The thing that changed under you

**Amazon gutted the Associates program in spring 2026.**

- **April 14, 2026** — onsite commissions now count only the **promoted ASIN or
  its variants**. The old "halo" model, where you earned on anything else the
  shopper bought in that session, is gone.
- **May 2026** — commission rates cut by **up to 50%** across categories,
  milestone and year-over-year bonuses removed, and reporting tools degraded.
- Most everyday categories now pay **1–3%**.

One deal-site publisher expects 2026 Amazon revenue **50% below projection**.

**This explains your numbers exactly.** 8 orders → $6.46 = **$0.81 per item**.
That is not a content problem or a conversion problem. Your conversion rate is
11.76%, which is excellent. You are being paid almost nothing per sale because
the program was restructured.

**You are optimizing the wrong variable.** More videos → more clicks on the
worst-paying affiliate program in the market is not a path to money.

## Recommended order

### 1. UGC for brands — start this week
Brands pay **$150–400 per video** for content they run as their own ads. No
audience required. You already make product videos daily; you are giving that
skill away for $0.81 a sale.

Your 4.6K Instagram following is social proof, not the product. The videos are
the product.

Beginners charge $75–300, mid-tier $300–1,000. Start at $150–200/video, raise
after five paid jobs. Volume commitments cut rates 15–30%, so quote per-video
first.

### 2. TikTok Shop affiliate — swap the payer, keep the content
US average commission is **13.02%** against Amazon's 1–3%. Sellers set their
own rates (1–80%). Open Collaboration runs 10–15%; Targeted Collaboration
15–25% once you have a track record.

Identical videos. Four to six times the payout.

### 3. Amazon Influencer on-site videos — the compounding asset
Upload once, it lives on the product page permanently and earns base commission
plus a 1–5% bonus on anything bought within 24 hours of the view. This is the
one Amazon play still worth doing, because it does not depend on your feed.

### 4. Amazon Associates off-site — keep, stop investing in
Your bio.site links still earn. But stop treating this as the business.

## On the clothing / try-on idea

The category is not the problem. The **program** is.

- Amazon fashion pays ~4% before the cuts
- TikTok Shop fashion typically runs 15–20%
- Apparel carries **high return rates**, and returns claw back commissions
- It requires a real person wearing items **every single day** — a large
  operational lift, and the AI-presenter version already collapsed your
  Instagram reach by ~95%

Moving to clothes on Amazon means working harder for the same bad rate.
Moving your **existing** home/kitchen content to TikTok Shop means the same
work for 4–6x. Do that first.
