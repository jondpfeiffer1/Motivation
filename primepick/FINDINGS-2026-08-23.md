## Three problems to fix

### 1. The funnel leak is worse than it looked
~12,000 YouTube views in August, plus Instagram plays — and Amazon logged
**68 clicks this month**. That is roughly a **0.5%** content-to-Amazon rate.
Views are not the problem. The path from video to tagged link is broken.

### 2. Engagement caps distribution
2,789 views → 23 likes → **0 comments**. Across nearly every video, comments
are zero. Comments are the strongest ranking signal on Shorts and Reels.
Every video needs a question that is easy to answer.

### 3. One video was not distributed
"We were spending $80 a month on bottled water. EIGHTY." (Aug 15)
— **1 view on YouTube**, while videos on either side got 628–1,668.
On Instagram the same reel got 172 plays. That is a distribution failure,
not an audience verdict. Worth re-uploading as a fresh video.
