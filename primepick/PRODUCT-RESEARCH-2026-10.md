# Product research — 17 Oct 2026

Sources: vidIQ outlier search (Instagram + TikTok, last 30 days, 150K+ views,
ranked by multiple of the creator's own median), AmzScout Home & Kitchen
revenue ranking (12 Oct 2026), and current cleantok coverage.

Selection filter — every pick must fit the locked recipe:
1. Single product, demonstrable hands-only
2. A visible state change for beat 3 (fill, fold, absorb, clean, transform)
3. In niche: home, kitchen, cleaning, organization, pets, family
4. Ideally seasonal for mid-October, or higher-ticket

Compilation videos (5 products in one) were excluded as formats — the account
makes one product per video.

## Caveats
- Prices, ASINs and Amazon availability are unverified — Amazon is blocked
  from this environment.
- The coat-rack outliers were tagged #tiktokshop; confirm an Amazon listing
  exists before committing.
- Branded products (Ninja, The Pink Stuff) are easiest to find exactly; generic
  ones (mat, rack, ironing board) have many near-identical listings — pick the
  one with the most reviews.
